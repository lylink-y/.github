# Lylink

It has long been said that the best way to learn is by doing. Lylink is built on that belief.

We are an open-source collective for people who want to grow by building and maintaining projects together. 
We do it because we like it and believe it is worth it. 

Some of our projects will be essentially educational: opportunities to experiment and practice the theory. 
Others, we hope, will grow beyond that: into tools and resources that make a difference out there in the world.

Like the flower from which we take our name and identity, we hope to grow where others might not expect us to and to bloom in our own time.
