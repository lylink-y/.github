# Governance

This document describes how Lylink is organized and governed. It defines the roles within the organization, the responsibilities and authority associated with each, and the principles by which decisions are made.

## Roles

Roles exist to distribute responsibility and authority according to each person's level of involvement and the responsibilities they have taken on. 
The hierarchy, from highest to lowest, is as follows:

- **Founder**: has final authority over Lylink and is ultimately responsible for the organization.
- **Wardens**: help administer Lylink as a whole, overseeing its policies and their enforcement.
- **Keepers**: are responsible for individual projects. They maintain their project's repository, coordinate its development, and review and integrate contributions.
- **Members**: are full participants in the organization and are associated with a team. They contribute to the development, maintenance, or other work related to the projects they are part of.

## Decision-Making

Lylink follows a simple principle:

> **Whenever possible, decisions should be made at the lowest level capable of making them.**

Higher-level roles may, when appropriate, intervene in matters normally handled at a lower level, particularly to enforce compliance or act in the interests of the organization.

## Appointments and Removal

Roles are granted based on contribution, responsibility, and trust. Seniority may be taken into consideration, but is not necessarily determinative.

Roles are generally appointed and removed by the role immediately above them in the hierarchy. They may be removed when their responsibilities are no longer being fulfilled or when circumstances otherwise require it.

## Enforcement

Violations of Lylink's rules or Code of Conduct may result in warnings, restrictions, removal of roles or access, suspension, or removal from Lylink.
