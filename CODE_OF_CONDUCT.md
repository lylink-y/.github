# Code of Conduct

Lylink is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in our community and projects.

We expect participants to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives.
- Give and accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on improving the project rather than winning arguments.
- Respect the boundaries and privacy of other participants.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

Violations of Lylink's rules or Code of Conduct may result in warnings, restrictions, removal of roles or access, suspension, or permanent removal from Lylink, at a Warden's discretion.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the Lylink Wardens.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
