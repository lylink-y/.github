# Contributing to Lylink

Lylink is built by people learning and building together.

## Finding a Project

Browse the [Lylink projects](https://github.com/lylink-y) or visit our Discord community to find something that interests you.

If a project looks interesting, start by reading its `README`.

## Contributing

Projects may use different languages, tools, and workflows, so always follow the project's own contribution guidelines where they exist.

In general:

1. Look through the project and its documentation.
2. Check existing issues and discussions.
3. Ask questions when something is unclear.
4. Discuss larger changes before investing significant work in them.
5. Open a reasonably sized pull request with a clear description of what you changed and why.
6. Be willing to discuss and revise your contribution.

Not every contribution needs to be code. You can also contribute by improving documentation, testing and reporting bugs, or helping other contributors. For security vulnerabilities, please refer to our [Security Guidelines](docs/SECURITY.md).

## Proposing a Project

Have an idea of your own? Propose it in the Lylink Discord community.

A proposal should explain what the project is, why it is interesting or useful, what technologies it might use, its first milestone, and how you intend to contribute.

Not every proposal will become an official Lylink project. Ideas may be revised, put on hold, or rejected, and none of those outcomes prevent you from pursuing the idea elsewhere.

## LLMs and AI tools

You are free to use LLMs and other AI tools as part of your work. They can be useful for learning, exploring ideas, debugging, and navigating unfamiliar code.

However, contributions should ultimately be understood and owned by the person submitting them. Don't submit generated code that you cannot explain, review, or maintain yourself.

## A Note on Collaboration

Lylink is intended to be a welcoming place to learn. You don't need to know everything before contributing, and asking questions is part of the process.
