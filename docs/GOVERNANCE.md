# Governance

## Roles

Lylink uses distinct role names to make responsibilities and areas of authority clear and give them a bit more character. We deliberately avoid the lengthy or bland titles used in corporations; e.g., a **Keeper** is roughly what might elsewhere be called a **Project Lead**.

* **Founder** — Has overall responsibility for the organization and authority over its direction.
* **Warden** — Helps administer the organization: its policies, channels, repositories, and teams.
* **Keeper** — Responsible for the direction and maintenance of an individual project.
* **Member** — A member of a project team who is committed to its development and maintenance.

Those who contribute to or participate in Lylink in some capacity without holding a formal organizational role are called **Associates**.

## Administration

The Founder may appoint or remove anyone within the organizational hierarchy. In practice, Keeper appointments and removals are generally left to a Warden, while Member appointments and removals are generally left to the relevant Keeper.

Appointments and removals are based on the individual's contributions, [conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), reliability, and ability to fulfill the responsibilities of the role.
