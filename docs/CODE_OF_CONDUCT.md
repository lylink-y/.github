# Code of Conduct

1. **Be respectful.** Treat others with respect. Harassment, personal attacks, and needless hostility are not acceptable.
2. **Build in good faith.** Contribute with the intention of learning, creating, maintaining, and helping others.
3. **Keep it constructive.** Criticism and disagreement are welcome when they are relevant and expressed constructively.
4. **Give credit.** Respect the work and contributions of others. Do not claim someone else's work as your own.
5. **Follow the rules.** Follow GitHub's Terms of Service, as well as the policies and guidelines of our organization and its projects.
6. **Ask for help when needed.** If you're unsure whether something is appropriate, ask a Keeper for project-level matters or a Warden for organization-level matters.

## Reporting

If you witness a serious policy violation that cannot be resolved amicably, raise the matter privately with the appropriate Keeper. If the matter cannot be resolved at the project level, or if the Keeper is involved in the matter, escalate it privately to a Warden.

## Sanctions

Violations of this Code of Conduct or other applicable policies may result in appropriate action, depending on their severity and circumstances. Actions may include warnings, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or removal from a project or the organization.

Serious or repeated violations may result in stronger action without prior warnings.
